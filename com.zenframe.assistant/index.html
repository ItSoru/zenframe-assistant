Settings

AI provider

Bring your own AI. Zenframe has no servers: requests go straight from your computer to the provider you choose, under your own account and its pricing or free tier.

✓Tested and works best with Claude Code.

Sign in with Claude Code itself: open Command Prompt and run claude auth login, then finish the sign-in in your browser. Zenframe never sees or stores your login.

Codex runs on your ChatGPT plan (Plus, Pro or Team), so no API key is needed. Install it once with npm install -g @openai/codex, then sign in: open Command Prompt, run codex login and choose Sign in with ChatGPT. Zenframe never sees or stores your login.

Each provider's key is encrypted for your Windows account and only sent to that provider. It is never shown, logged, or sent anywhere else.

Zeni needs a model that supports tool calling. Free and small models are less reliable at After Effects edits than larger ones, so keep edit approval on.

Safety

On: every batch of edits is shown first with Apply and Decline. Off: edits apply right away (each batch is still one Undo step). You can also switch this under the message box.

Your editing style

Pick your styles and add anything specific. Zeni reads the matching style guide before giving feedback, so it judges your edits on your style's terms. Saved on this computer.

Project files (.aep)

Zeni can study any After Effects project file without opening it, even one that crashes After Effects. Use the paperclip > Study a project file, or paste the file's path. This needs Python 3 and the free py-aep library.

Accent color

Change the highlight color.

Zeni

Zeni sits on the message box and shows what the AI is doing in a thought bubble. Drag him anywhere: drop him near the top of the tab bars, the Conversation/Scripts switch or the line under the Zenframe title and he sits on it. Double-click him to send him home. Turn this off for the plain activity line.

Music to dance to

Open a comp to see its sound layers.

When you play a comp with music, Zeni vibes to the beat: a chill sway for slow songs (under 105 BPM), head bobbing for faster ones. The tempo is worked out on this computer from the song file, including time stretch. While he is busy he keeps his working pose and just bounces.

Voice

Click the mic in the message box, talk, then click it again. Your words are typed into the box; nothing is sent until you press Send.

Wallpaper

Show an image behind the panel with a glass look. It is darkened just enough that text always stays readable, and it stays on this computer.

Colors from your image (click one to use it as the accent):

Text gets a dark outline to stay legible. On very bright images, lower this if text gets hard to read.
